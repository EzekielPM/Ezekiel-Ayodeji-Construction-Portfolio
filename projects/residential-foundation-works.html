Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

This residential project focused on the foundation stage, including excavation, reinforcement-intensive work, pile-cap and ground-beam construction and preparation for the superstructure.

My Role

Site Manager

As Site Manager, I coordinated foundation execution, interpreted structural details, managed reinforcement and concrete operations, coordinated site teams and maintained dimensional and quality control.

Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

Foundation-stage delivery required close attention to reinforcement placement, levels, dimensions and structural interfaces before concrete placement and subsequent work.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

Excavation, reinforcement, formwork and concrete operations were sequenced so that the site remained ready for checking, inspection and the next stage of construction.

Quality & Execution

Quality protected during the work.

Foundation work leaves little room for correction later, so checks had to happen before work was concealed or concrete made errors difficult to reverse.
