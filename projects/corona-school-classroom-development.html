Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

The Corona School project involved construction of classroom facilities in Ikoyi, progressing through excavation, foundation work, reinforced-concrete framing, slab and formwork operations and masonry.

My Role

Site Engineer

As Site Engineer, I supported structural execution, interpreted drawings and bar-bending schedules, coordinated workers and monitored site quality and progress.

Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

I interpreted structural information, checked reinforcement and formwork preparation, and coordinated execution against drawings so that structural activities could progress accurately.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

I worked closely with site labour and trades to keep structural activities sequenced and focused on the required delivery programme.

Quality & Execution
