Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

Jubilee Chalet involved renovation and repair work within an existing residential and hospitality property. Unlike a new-build site, the work required understanding existing conditions, identifying areas requiring intervention and coordinating corrective works without treating the building as a blank canvas.

My Role

Site Engineer / Project Manager

As Site Engineer / Project Manager, I coordinated assessment, repair and renovation activities, site teams, sequencing and the quality of corrective work.

Scope of Work

Key workstreams.

Existing-condition review
Repair works
Renovation activities
Finishing corrections
Coordination of trades around retained building elements
Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

The technical challenge was to translate observed site conditions into practical corrective actions while protecting retained elements and ensuring that new interventions responded properly to the existing property.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

Repairs and finishing activities had to be sequenced carefully to reduce rework and disruption within an established building environment.

Quality & Execution

Quality protected during the work.

Particular attention was paid to interfaces between retained work and new interventions, because renovation quality depends heavily on how well those boundaries are resolved.
