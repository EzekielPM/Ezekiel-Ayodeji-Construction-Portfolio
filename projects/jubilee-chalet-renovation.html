Project Overview

Repairing an existing property without creating new problems.

Jubilee Chalet was not a blank-site project. Existing finishes, services and building elements had to be assessed before intervention, with repair decisions shaped by what could remain, what required correction and how new work would connect to the retained property.

My Role

Site Engineer / Project Manager

I coordinated the condition review, defined practical work sequences, organised repair trades and monitored corrective work through completion. My role required balancing immediate site findings with the need to protect retained elements and avoid unnecessary disruption.

Technical Delivery

Diagnosing conditions before choosing an intervention.

Observed defects were considered in relation to the surrounding building rather than treated in isolation. Corrective actions were planned to address the affected area while preserving sound work and ensuring that new materials and details responded appropriately to existing conditions.

Site Coordination

Sequencing repair trades around retained finishes.

Access, removal, repair and reinstatement were organised in a controlled order. This reduced avoidable damage, limited repeated work and allowed different trades to operate without undoing one another's progress.

Quality & Execution

Making repaired work belong to the existing building.

Quality checks focused on the junctions between old and new work, the completeness of corrections and the consistency of the reinstated finishes. A successful repair needed to perform properly and sit naturally within the retained property.
