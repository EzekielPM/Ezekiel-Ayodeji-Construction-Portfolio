Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

The Country Home project was a substantial residential development progressing from structural frame and blockwork through roofing, finishes, external works and completion. The project required sustained coordination across multiple construction phases over an extended delivery period.

My Role

Site / Project Manager

As Site / Project Manager, I led day-to-day delivery, coordinated structural, masonry, roofing and finishing activities, managed site teams and resources, and monitored quality through execution.

Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

I maintained continuity between drawings and site execution across structural and architectural stages, with particular attention to the sequencing and coordination required around the roof structure and later finishing work.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

Trades, labour and resources had to be coordinated as the project moved from heavy construction into finishing and external works, with each stage prepared for the next without losing control of quality.

Quality & Execution

Quality protected during the work.

Workmanship was monitored progressively and completed work protected as the project transitioned between structural, roofing, finishing and external-work stages.
