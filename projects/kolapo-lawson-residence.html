Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

The Kolapo Lawson Residence involved partial demolition and reconstruction of an existing residential building. The work required careful coordination between retained building elements and new structural components.

My Role

Site Engineer

As Site Engineer, I coordinated demolition and reconstruction activities, interpreted structural requirements, supervised reinforcement and structural work, managed site teams and supported quality control.

Scope of Work

Key workstreams.

Partial demolition
New foundations and structural elements
Reinforcement and concrete work
Masonry
Roofing and reconstruction coordination
Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

New structural work had to interface properly with retained elements, with sequencing planned to protect the integrity of the existing structure while introducing new work.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

Demolition and rebuild stages were coordinated so that each new intervention followed logically from what had been retained or removed.

Quality & Execution

Quality protected during the work.

Structural interfaces, dimensions and workmanship were monitored through the reconstruction process to maintain alignment between existing and new work.

Project Journey

Site documentation.

What This Project Demonstrates

Reconstruction demands more judgement than simply following a new-build sequence because every new intervention must respond to an existing condition.
