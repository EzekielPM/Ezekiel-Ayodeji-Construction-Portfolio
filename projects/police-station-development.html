Project Overview

Context and delivery environment.

The Police Station Development involved three parallel structures within one public-sector site. Delivery required simultaneous coordination of structural and masonry activities across several work fronts, with site resources, teams and sequencing managed as one programme rather than as isolated buildings.

My Role

Site / Project Manager

As Site / Project Manager, I led day-to-day site and project coordination across the three structures, aligning teams, work sequencing, material needs, structural activities and quality expectations.

Scope of Work

Key workstreams.

Reinforced-concrete works
Reinforcement and formwork
Blockwork and masonry
Roofing progression
Workforce and resource coordination across three structures
Technical Delivery

Turning technical intent into site execution.

I interpreted project drawings, coordinated structural activities, monitored reinforcement, formwork and concrete-related work, and maintained consistency across parallel structures as they progressed through different construction stages.

Site Coordination

Keeping work fronts aligned.

The project required balancing labour, resources and priorities across multiple work fronts, anticipating dependencies and ensuring that progress on one structure did not disrupt the wider programme.

Quality & Execution

Quality protected during the work.

Execution standards had to remain consistent across all structures while progress, sequencing and site issues were tracked and addressed across the wider development.

Site documentation.

What This Project Demonstrates

Leadership across multiple structures demands programme-level thinking — not just supervision of individual tasks.
