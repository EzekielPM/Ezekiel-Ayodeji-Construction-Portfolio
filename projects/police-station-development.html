Project Overview

Managing three structures as one delivery programme.

The development comprised three buildings progressing simultaneously within the same public-sector site. Because the structures were not always at the same stage, delivery required a programme-wide view of labour, materials, inspections and next activities rather than treating each building as a separate job.

My Role

Site / Project Manager

I coordinated daily execution across all three structures, set work priorities, allocated teams and resources, reviewed material requirements and monitored progress against the wider delivery sequence. I also resolved site issues and maintained communication between the different work fronts.

Technical Delivery

Maintaining technical consistency across three buildings.

I interpreted drawings and supervised reinforcement, formwork, concrete and masonry activities across the development. Checks were coordinated according to the stage of each structure so that technical standards remained consistent even when the buildings progressed at different rates.

Site Coordination

Balancing parallel work fronts.

Labour and materials had to move between structures without creating avoidable idle time. I anticipated upcoming dependencies, adjusted priorities around site readiness and kept each team focused on work that supported the overall programme.

Quality & Execution

Applying one quality standard across the development.

Reinforcement, formwork, concrete and masonry were checked before subsequent work concealed them. The same expectations for dimensions, workmanship and drawing compliance were maintained across all three structures.
