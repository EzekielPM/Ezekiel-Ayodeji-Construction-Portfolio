Professional Profile

Engineering judgement backed by real site delivery.

My construction career has been built around one central responsibility: turning drawings, plans and resources into finished work on site.

Over the years, I have worked across structural construction, renovation, commercial developments, residential projects and public infrastructure, taking responsibility for both technical execution and the coordination required to deliver effectively.

My experience includes supervising reinforcement, formwork, concrete operations, masonry, roofing, finishing and foundation works; interpreting structural and architectural drawings; coordinating artisans and site teams; managing materials and resources; monitoring quality; resolving site issues; and maintaining alignment between project requirements and actual execution.

As my responsibilities grew from Site Engineer to Site Manager and Project Manager, my role expanded beyond supervising individual activities. I became increasingly responsible for sequencing work, coordinating multiple teams, managing dependencies, supporting programme delivery and making practical decisions where time, quality, cost and site realities had to be balanced.
